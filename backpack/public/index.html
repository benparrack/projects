Backpack

Connect your school calendar

Paste your calendar feed link once. Your classes and assignments then show up here and stay up to date by themselves.

  • Canvas: open Calendar, click Calendar Feed (bottom right) and copy the link.
  • Schoology: open Calendar, choose Export / iCal feed and copy the link.
  • Others (Google Classroom, Brightspace, Blackboard…): look for an iCal or "subscribe to calendar" link.

The link stays on this device. It is only used to fetch your calendar.
