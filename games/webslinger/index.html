Controls

Hold left mouse / Shift swing — keep holding to chain, let go on the upswing to fly
Space jump (hold to charge) · jump off a swing for a boost
Aim the white ring shows where your web will stick. Look at a pole tip (◇ antennas, spires, rooftop masts) and the ring turns gold: your swing locks onto it
Right mouse / E zip to the ledge you aim at and launch
Q web-dash (3 charges) · Ctrl / C dive
F air trick: W front flip · S backflip · A/D corkscrew · none 360. Land before it finishes and you bail
Tab / middle mouse hold for slow-mo (blue bar)
G drop in at the next ring race (again to cancel) · gold beacons mark the starts
M map · T time of day · V look · P hide HUD · R respawn · F3 stats

Style

Everything off the ground scores: swings, big flings, tricks, near misses, close calls, low swoops, wall runs, tokens, rings. The multiplier grows as you chain moves; the combo banks when you stay on your feet, and is lost if you bail or fall in the river.
