# LANTERN — script

**Logline:** A small, failing probe that has crossed deep space calling out
with no reply finds a colossal dead gate orbiting a gas giant. Its call wakes
the gate, which opens a way through to the place where every other lantern
went.

**Runtime target:** 2:56 (176 s). Wordless. Picture is letterboxed 2.39:1 inside a 1920×1080 frame.

**Characters and focal objects**
- **Lantern:** a one-metre probe. It has a slotted spherical cage around a warm
  amber core, an equatorial ring, two whip antennas and one lens "eye". Its
  core light shows how it's doing: a steady glow means healthy, a flicker
  means failing, and dark means it has been knocked out. It talks by *pinging*:
  it sends out an expanding shell of light with a two-note call (a rising
  fifth: A4 → E5).
- **The Gate:** a ring 6,000 km across, dark and machined, in orbit around a
  banded gas giant. Its inner face is a canyon of panels and towers between
  two rim walls. It is dead until it hears the call.

**Motif:** The two-note ping is the whole score in miniature. Lantern calls
(A→E). The Gate answers the same interval an octave lower. The swarm on the
other side answers it as a chord across the whole spectrum.
