# Roblox market research (2026-09-28)

Condensed from a web-research pass. Blog and tracker figures are third-party and approximate.

## Discovery algorithm
- **June 2026:** "Recommended For You" measures retention over 28 days in three phases: day 1, days 2–7 and days 8–28.
- The old click-then-play metric (QPTR) was split into separate play-through, session-quality and spending signals.
- The Aug 2026 update rewards games strong on both retention and spending.
- Weekly updates matter more than occasional big ones. Fix retention before buying ads.

## Money
- **Revenue share:** 70% of game pass and developer product prices.
- **Price points:** impulse passes at 25–199 R$, 2x passes at 99–199 R$.
- **DevEx:** $0.0038/R$, or $0.0054/R$ for spending by verified US adults since June 8, 2026.
  - Needs 30,000 R$ minimum, age 13+, a verified email, and a W-8 tax form (non-US).
- **Creator Rewards (replaced Premium Payouts in 2025):** 5 R$/day per "active spender" who plays your game for 10+ minutes as one of their first 3 games that day.
- **Rewarded video ads:** open to ads-eligible creators since Feb 2026. About $6–12 per 1,000 views, and about 8–15% of revenue.

## Policies
- **Publish publicly:** account at least 2 days old, an age check, and the content maturity questionnaire.
- **Reach under-16s:** also 2FA, plus either 2 consecutive months of Premium or a refundable 1,000 R$ fee (~$12.50) per game.
- **Paid random items:** all outcomes and odds must be shown before purchase, including luck boosts.
- **Limit:** up to 5 new games made public per day.
