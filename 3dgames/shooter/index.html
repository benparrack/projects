3D Shooter

A round-based wave shooter. Clear enemies each round to earn gold, then spend it in the shop on better weapons, armor, perks and upgrades before the next round starts. Every 5th round is a boss fight.

WASD move   Mouse look   Click shoot   R reload   1-5 weapons   6 knife   Shift dash (if bought)   Right-click zoom (sniper)   Esc pause

Rounds 1-2 are melee-only so you can get your bearings. Ranged gunners appear from round 3, and snipers telegraph with a red laser before firing — break line of sight to dodge them. Regular gun shots are real dodgeable projectiles, not instant hits, so keep moving.
