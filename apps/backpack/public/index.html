Backpack

Your homework, already filled in.

Connect your school calendar once. Every class and assignment shows up here and stays up to date by itself. No typing it all in.

1 · Copy your calendar link

Canvas

On a computer (the Canvas phone app doesn't show it), open Calendar in Canvas, click Calendar Feed at the bottom of the right sidebar, and copy the link in the box that pops up. Afterwards, Settings has a link to set up your phone in one tap.

Schoology

Open Calendar, click the Export / iCal button and copy the link. Schoology doesn't say which class each assignment is for, so you'll sort them into classes once.

Something else

Google Classroom, Brightspace, Blackboard, Moodle and most others have an "iCal", "export" or "subscribe to calendar" link in their calendar page.

2 · Paste it here

Pasting the link connects right away. Your link and your data stay on this device.
